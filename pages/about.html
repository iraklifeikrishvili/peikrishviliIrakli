About us

Climate Change IT is at the forefront of leveraging technology for environmental sustainability. Founded by a team of passionate experts, we strive to make a measurable impact on climate change.

Our story

Founded by Sarah Thompson, Climate Change IT emerged from a desire to merge technology with environmental advocacy. With a background in both computer science and environmental policy, Sarah and her dedicated team work tirelessly to educate communities and instill a sense of responsibility for our planet.
